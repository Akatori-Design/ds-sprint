Before you book

We don’t have a designer. Is this for us?

Yes. The system turns design decisions into tokens and written rules, so engineers and AI tools produce consistent UI without a designer reviewing every screen.

We don’t use Figma.

That’s fine. Code is the source of truth, so you design and build directly in code.

Do we need an existing design system?

No. We start from whatever you have: a live product, a component folder, a brand guide or a few screens. Days 1 and 2 turn it into a plan.

What do we need to prepare?

Access to your repo and any design files, plus someone who can make product decisions on days 1 and 2 and join the handover on day 10.

Where does the $200k+ figure come from?

It’s a typical startup: one designer and two UI engineers on a $150k average salary. The system halves the time they spend on design, handoff and building UI, which frees about 60 hours a week: about $225k a year in salary. Use the calculator above with your own numbers.

What happens after the sprint?

You own everything: the code, the tokens and the docs. The agent rules keep the system consistent as your team ships.
